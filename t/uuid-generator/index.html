UUID Generator

One v4 UUID, copied to your clipboard the moment you press Generate. v7 embeds a millisecond timestamp in the high bits so IDs sort chronologically — much kinder to database indexes — and v5 derives a stable UUID from a name.

Open UUID Generator →

What it does

Generate UUID v4, time-ordered v7, or v1-style identifiers in bulk. It runs entirely in your browser — your input never leaves your machine, so it is safe to use with data you would not paste into a hosted service.

How to use it

  1. Open UUID Generator and paste or type your input.
  2. Adjust the options if you need something other than the defaults.
  3. Copy the result, or save it to a file.

Frequently asked questions

Is UUID Generator free to use?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free, needs no account, and has no usage limits.

Does UUID Generator upload my data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your input is never sent to a server, which makes it safe for data you would not paste into a hosted tool. The two exceptions across the whole site (the Azure Service Bus relay and the IP lookup) are documented and opt-in.

What options does UUID Generator have?

You can configure: Version, How many, Copy to clipboard automatically, Names for v3/v5 (comma separated), Namespace for v3/v5.

Does UUID Generator work offline?

Once the page has loaded it keeps working without a connection. A few tools fetch a library on first use, after which they are cached.
