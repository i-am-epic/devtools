Things worth knowing

Typed here, this never leaves your browser — but as a rule, do not paste a password you actually use into any website. Test the pattern, not the real thing. The strongest practical advice remains: a long passphrase from a password manager, unique per site, with 2FA on top.

Frequently asked questions

Is Password Strength Checker free to use?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free, needs no account, and has no usage limits.

Does Password Strength Checker upload my data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your input is never sent to a server, which makes it safe for data you would not paste into a hosted tool. The two exceptions across the whole site (the Azure Service Bus relay and the IP lookup) are documented and opt-in.
