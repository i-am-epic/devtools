Kubernetes Quantity Converter

Mi is 1024-based, M is 1000-based, and mixing them up is how you end up with a pod that OOMs. One line per quantity.

Open Kubernetes Quantity Converter →

What it does

Translate 512Mi, 1.5Gi, 250m and friends into numbers you can reason about. It runs entirely in your browser — your input never leaves your machine, so it is safe to use with data you would not paste into a hosted service.

Example input

512Mi
1.5Gi
2G
250m
1500m
4

How to use it

  1. Open Kubernetes Quantity Converter and paste or type your input.
  2. The result appears as you type.
  3. Copy the result, or save it to a file.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kubernetes Quantity Converter free to use?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free, needs no account, and has no usage limits.

Does Kubernetes Quantity Converter upload my data?

No. It runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your input is never sent to a server, which makes it safe for data you would not paste into a hosted tool. The two exceptions across the whole site (the Azure Service Bus relay and the IP lookup) are documented and opt-in.

Does Kubernetes Quantity Converter work offline?

Once the page has loaded it keeps working without a connection. A few tools fetch a library on first use, after which they are cached.
